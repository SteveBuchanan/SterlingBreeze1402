The condo

Room for everyone, with the Gulf right outside

Sterling Breeze unit 1402 sleeps eight. There's a master suite with its own full bath, a bunk area with two bunks, a pull-out sleeper sofa and a second full bath. Cook in the full kitchen, keep beach towels fresh with the in-unit washer and dryer, and end the day on the Gulf-view balcony.
